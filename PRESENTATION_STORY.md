# Monsun Bridge — presentation working script
Status: narrative ready to adapt; empirical findings not yet validated. Do not read bracketed instructions as results.

## 0:00–0:45 · Team and question
We are [registered team name]. Our project is Monsun Bridge.
When a coastal tourism destination enters a quiet season, the planning question is not only how many visitors arrive. It is how to prepare the people whose livelihoods depend on that activity.
Monsun Bridge connects seasonal signals, workforce context and evidence-based transition planning.

## 0:45–1:45 · Purpose and boundary
We focus on Besut, Kuala Nerus and Marang, with Rompin as an extension case.
This is decision support for public-sector coordination. It is not a job guarantee or an individual-income-loss calculator.
Our primary outcome is a more transparent decision: who should be assessed, what routes need verification and when preparation should begin.
