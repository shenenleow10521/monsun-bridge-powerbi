# Monsun Bridge Power BI Redesign

## Four-page visual story
1. **P1 · WHEN** — dynamic Monsoon Clock, KPI row, hero chart, verified monsoon windows and resolution guardrail.
2. **P2 · WHO** — exposure KPIs, context-only locator, ranked district/occupation views and modelled-allocation table.
3. **P3 · WHERE TO** — filter rail, ranked pathways, Transition Card KPIs, skill evidence and explainability panel.
4. **P4 · WHAT ACTION** — controls, six KPIs, cost/exposure comparison, district allocation, disclaimer and action timeline.

Palette: deep navy, sea teal, warning amber and coral on a pale policy-report background.

## Data integrity
- Input folder: `C:\MonsunBridge\data\processed`.
- Processed files use `MB_*.csv`.
- Only verified METMalaysia seasons are embedded.
- National quarterly tourism is OBSERVED.
- State/district quarterly allocation, WME, transition scores, capacity proxies and programme costs are MODELLED.

This environment cannot execute Power BI Desktop. Complete `POWER_BI_VALIDATION_CHECKLIST.md` on Windows. Keep `.pbi/cache.abf` local.
